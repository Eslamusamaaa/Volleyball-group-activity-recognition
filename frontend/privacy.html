Privacy policy

VolleyAI is an educational, non-commercial project that recognises volleyball group and player activity in video. Last updated 4 November 2026.

What we collect

The site is open to everyone and needs no account. These are recorded when you use it, whether or not you sign in:

  • Visit details. The time, your browser and system type, your approximate location (country, region and city, derived from your connection), your IP address shortened by its last part (for example 203.0.113.0), your language, time zone and screen size, and the page or campaign link you came from.
  • A random browser ID stored in your browser, so repeat visits can be counted and, if you later sign in, linked to your email address.
  • How you use the page. How long you stay, how far you scroll, which sections you view and how many times you click.

If you choose to sign in with Google (needed only to run an analysis) we also receive:

  • Your Google email address and whether Google has verified it. We do not receive your password, contacts or any other Google data.
  • Which videos you analyse (the file name of an upload or of a library video) and when.
  • Videos you upload. They are processed to produce the annotated result and are deleted from the server afterwards.

Why

To understand how many people use the project and how, to protect the compute that analyses run on (only signed-in people can start one), and to keep a record of who does.

Sharing and retention

We do not sell or share this information with anyone. Usage records are kept for as long as the project runs. The service is hosted on Vercel (web pages) and Modal (analysis server).

Your choices

You can sign out at any time, clear the browser ID by clearing this site's data in your browser, and revoke the site's access from your Google account settings. To ask for your records to be deleted, open an issue on the project's GitHub page.
