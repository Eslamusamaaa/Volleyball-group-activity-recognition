Privacy policy

VolleyAI is an educational, non-commercial project that recognises volleyball group and player activity in video. Last updated 4 November 2026.

What we collect

  • Your Google email address. When you sign in with Google we receive your email address and its verification status from Google. We do not receive your password, contacts or any other Google data.
  • Usage records. The time you signed in, and which video you asked us to analyse (the file name of an upload or of a library video).
  • Videos you upload. They are processed to produce the annotated result and are deleted from the server afterwards.

Why

Only signed-in people can use the site, and we keep a record of who does, to control access to the service and to understand how it is used.

Sharing and retention

We do not sell or share this information with anyone. Usage records are kept for as long as the project runs. The service is hosted on Vercel (web pages) and Modal (analysis server).

Your choices

You can sign out at any time, and you can revoke the site's access from your Google account settings. To ask for your records to be deleted, open an issue on the project's GitHub page.
